## What it is
A platform for collecting, managing and displaying customer testimonials. Users gather testimonials through a dedicated landing page, organise them in an interactive dashboard, and embed them on their own websites with customisable widgets.

## The problem
Praise from customers ends up scattered across emails, chats and screenshots, and most of it never reaches the website where it could help. Small businesses need one place to collect testimonials, tidy them up and show them without asking a developer for help.

## What I built
A public page where a customer submits a testimonial, a dashboard where the owner reviews and organises what came in, and an embeddable widget that shows the chosen testimonials on any site. The API is Node and Express on MongoDB; the dashboard and the widget are React.

## What was hard
Making the widget look right on pages it knows nothing about. Keeping its styles from clashing with the host page, while keeping the embed small, took several rounds of testing on different sites.

## Result
A working flow from submission to embed. Next steps would be moderation tools and a few more widget themes.
